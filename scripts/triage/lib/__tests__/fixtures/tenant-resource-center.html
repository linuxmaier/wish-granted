Eviction Prevention and Rent Assistance

The Tenant Resource Center runs the Eviction Prevention Coordinated Entry screening for Dane County. If you are behind on rent or have received an eviction notice, start with our housing help desk.

How it works

This is a pre-screening questionnaire, not an application. Completing it does not guarantee assistance and does not reserve funds. A housing counselor will review your situation and talk through your options, including ones that are not money.

Funding for rent assistance is limited and runs out at points during the year. Income limits and funding availability change and are not published here in a way you can check yourself — the only way to know whether you qualify is to contact a counselor and apply.

Tenant counseling is free and available to everyone regardless of income. Call the housing help desk at 608-257-0006, or start online.
