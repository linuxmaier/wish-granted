211 Wisconsin

211 Wisconsin is a free, confidential referral helpline that connects you to local assistance of any kind — rent help, food, utilities, childcare, health care. Dial 211 from any phone, any time, in any language.

A referral specialist searches a database of local programs with you and makes warm handoffs to the organizations that can help. Available 24 hours a day.

There are no eligibility requirements to call 211. It is a referral service, not a source of money itself. You do not need to qualify for anything first.

Dial 211, or text your ZIP code to 898211.
