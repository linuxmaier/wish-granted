Find Help

Second Harvest is a network of food pantries and mobile food distributions across a 16-county region of southern Wisconsin, including Dane County. Pantries are open to anyone who needs food.

Food Support Qualification Guidelines

No identification, proof of income, residency, citizenship, or other documentation is required at Second Harvest Foodbank food distribution sites, including mobile pantries.

There is no income test and no application. Individual pantries may set their own limits on how often you can visit.

Use the pantry finder to locate a site near you and check its hours. Our FoodShare outreach team can also help you apply for SNAP for free, over the phone, at 1-877-366-3635.
