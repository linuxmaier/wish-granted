Joining Forces for Families

Joining Forces for Families (JFF) places neighborhood social workers across Dane County. They help residents find and apply for whatever they need — rent help, food, utilities, childcare, health care.

The service is free and voluntary. You do not need to qualify for anything first, and there is no application form to start — a worker will simply talk through your situation with you.

Workers are assigned by neighborhood, so which office you contact depends on where you live. Use the office finder to reach the worker for your neighborhood.

Dane County Department of Human Services
